Getting Started

To create an account:

  1. Click on the "Sign Up" button on our homepage
  2. Enter your email address and create a password
  3. Verify your email address through the confirmation link
  4. Complete your profile setup
  5. Start using the platform!

Time required: Less than 2 minutes

Our platform works on:

  • Browsers: Chrome 80+, Firefox 75+, Safari 13+, Edge 80+
  • Operating Systems: Windows 10+, macOS 10.14+, Linux
  • Mobile: iOS 13+, Android 8+
  • Internet: Stable connection with at least 5 Mbps
  • RAM: Minimum 4GB recommended

Account & Billing

To reset your password:

  1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
  2. Enter your registered email address
  3. Check your email for the reset link (expires in 24 hours)
  4. Click the link and create a new password
  5. Log in with your new password

Note: If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Credit/Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express
  • Digital Wallets: PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay
  • Bank Transfer: For annual enterprise plans
  • Cryptocurrency: Bitcoin, Ethereum (select regions)

All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time with no cancellation fees. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period.

To cancel:

  1. Go to Settings → Billing
  2. Click "Cancel Subscription"
  3. Select a reason (optional)
  4. Confirm cancellation

You can reactivate your account anytime within 30 days to retain your data.
